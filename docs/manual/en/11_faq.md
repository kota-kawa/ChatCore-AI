# Frequently asked questions

## Why is part of the page still in another language?

Chats, memos, custom tasks, and community posts remain in their original language. Refresh once if an interface label did not update after changing the setting.

## Why did the AI answer in a different language?

An explicit language request and the language of your message take priority over the saved interface language. Ask the assistant to answer in the language you want.

## Are AI answers always correct?

No. Verify important medical, legal, financial, security, and current information with appropriate primary sources or professionals.

## Can I use Chat-Core AI without an account?

Basic guest chat may be available, but saving history, tasks, memos, profile settings, and other account features requires sign-in.
