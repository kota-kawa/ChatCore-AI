# Memos

## Save a response

Save a useful AI response as a memo and give it a descriptive title. Memo text is user content and is not automatically translated when you change the interface language.

## View and copy

Open recent memos to review or copy their contents. Check generated text before treating it as authoritative or forwarding it to others.

## Edit a memo

Saved memos open in a formatted reading view. Clicking or tapping the text does not change the
appearance of headings, emphasis, tables, or code blocks. Checklist boxes can still be toggled in place.

Press Edit to change the Markdown text and use the formatting buttons. Return to Formatted view
on a computer, or View on a phone, to read the memo. Switching preserves your text and undo history.
Changes in memo details save automatically, with save status shown at the top.

New memos open ready for input. Use Preview to check their appearance and Edit to keep writing.
Press Save when finished.

## Delete a memo and the trash

Deleting a memo moves it to the trash without asking, and a notice with an Undo button appears at the bottom of the screen. The notice fades after a few seconds, but you can still restore the memo from the trash afterwards. Archiving, and the bulk actions that move memos to the trash or archive them, show the same Undo.

- Open the trash from Trash in the sidebar on a computer, or from Trash under View and organize on a phone.
- Memos stay in the trash for 30 days and are then deleted permanently. Each memo shows how many days are left.
- Trashed memos cannot be opened or edited. Restore one to put it back in your list, or delete it permanently right away (a permanently deleted memo cannot be recovered). Empty trash deletes everything in the trash at once.
- In bulk mode you can select trashed memos and restore them or delete them permanently together.
- Moving a memo to the trash turns its sharing link off. Restoring the memo does not turn sharing back on, so share it again from its sharing settings if you still want to.

## Search memos

Type keywords in the search box to search titles and text. Words separated by spaces must all appear in a memo, and the search ignores upper and lower case and the difference between hiragana and katakana. In the list, the words that were found are highlighted in the title and the text preview.

## Share a memo

Creating a sharing link makes the selected memo accessible to anyone with that link. Review the complete memo for private information before sharing, and revoke the link when it is no longer needed.
