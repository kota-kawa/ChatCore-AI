# Prompt sharing

## Browse public prompts

Search or filter public prompts, open a prompt to inspect its content and examples, and add a useful prompt to chat. Community content remains in the language submitted by its author.

## Publish a prompt

Signed-in users can publish prompts and manage their own posts. Do not include personal data, confidential material, credentials, copyrighted content you cannot redistribute, or harmful instructions.

## Likes and sharing

Like prompts you want to find again and copy a public sharing link where available. Removing or unpublishing a prompt can affect existing links.
