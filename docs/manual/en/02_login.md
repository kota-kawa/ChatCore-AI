# Sign-in and account verification

## Sign in with email

Enter your email address on the sign-in page. Chat-Core AI sends a six-digit verification code in the language selected when you started the flow. Enter the code before it expires. Repeated failed attempts may temporarily block verification.

## Other sign-in methods

You may also use Google sign-in or a registered passkey when those options are available. A passkey uses your device's screen lock, fingerprint, or face authentication and does not expose that biometric information to Chat-Core AI.

## If the email does not arrive

Check the address and spam folder, wait briefly, and try again. Too many requests may trigger a temporary or daily sending limit. Never share a verification code with another person.
