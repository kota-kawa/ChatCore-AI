# Settings

## Language

Open Settings and choose **Language**. Select Japanese or English. The interface updates immediately and the choice is saved for future sessions. Before sign-in, Chat-Core AI may use a language cookie or your browser language.

## Profile and appearance

Edit your profile and optional context supplied to the AI. Appearance settings control light, dark, or system theme independently of language.

## Email address

Changing your email requires one code sent to the current address and another sent to the new address. The flow keeps the language selected when it began. If you did not request a change, ignore the email and review account security.

## Security and passkeys

Register or remove passkeys from the security section. Keep at least one sign-in method available before removing a credential.
